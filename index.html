Studio Policies & Directives

Player & Community Directives

Player Data Security Matrix (DPDP Act Compliance)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and IT Act of India, MSP Games enforces strict isolation of system telemetry. No persistent player profiling or unauthorized tracking is permitted inside our production code blocks. Analytical metrics are aggregated anonymously to maintain absolute privacy matrices.

Community Tester Program & Bug Bounty Matrix

Authorized community testers validating active builds are eligible for system grants, including complimentary access to official game drops. Furthermore, under our Vetted Vulnerability Program, players who identify critical engine bugs and submit comprehensive technical solutions can qualify for a data security reward of up to ₹50,000, subject to verification by our core engineering matrix.

Anti-Tamper, Modding & Protection Laws

Unsanctioned asset modifications, inverse engineering, or runtime debugging hooks applied to active deployment packages outside official SDK provisions are strictly prohibited. Violations automatically drop terminal engine connection routes and nullify software access rights.

Global Player Support Protocol

Our support grid operates localized ticketing routes to resolve transaction faults, deployment failure states, and asset recovery parameters. We guarantee a maximum verification window of 48 operational hours for any tier-1 service request.

Internal Operations & Enterprise Protocols

Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA Enforcement)

All internal workforce assets, contractors, and external partners operate under strict, binding Non-Disclosure Agreements pursuant to the Indian Contract Act. Leakage of unreleased source files, design documentation, narrative scripts, or internal telemetry logs will initiate instant terminal severance and legal enforcement arrays.

Work From Home (WFH) & Hybrid Security Directives

Remote employees must execute development tasks through secure virtual networks. Hardware units housing internal source matrices (Godot/Unreal projects) must remain localized to authorized developers, with strict logging protocols enforced on every commit array to prevent digital leakage.

Foreign Remote Contractor Integration

International remote workforce assets are classified under cross-border technical consultancy frameworks. Compliance with native cross-border data transfer directives is mandatory, and intellectual property generated remains 100% proprietary to MSP Games.

Vacation Matrix & Operational Downtime

To sustain engineering efficiency and counter burn-out vectors, the studio structures mandatory annual downtime allocations alongside statutory holiday blocks, managed dynamically via internal scheduling nodes to keep game production pipelines balanced.
