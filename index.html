ABOUT MSP GAMES

Born To Create Legacy

OUR VISION

At MSP Games, we are an India-based, PC and console-focused multi-genre studio on a mission to build the future of Indian gaming. We build immersive worlds where gameplay meets emotional intensity, breaking standard genre boundaries to construct interactive experiences that leave lasting impacts on gamers worldwide.

CORE TECHNOLOGY

Powered by custom Godot 4, volumetric dynamic lighting, photogrammetry texturing, and bespoke binaural spatial audio, our engine setups push visual fidelity to modern AAA standards.

INDEPENDENT SPIRIT

We operate with complete creative freedom. Every mechanic, story arc, and visual asset is meticulously handcrafted by passionate developers free from corporate interference.

STUDIO LEADERSHIP & CULTURE

Founded by Manas Vatsh and co-founded with Daksh Yadav with a vision to create uncompromising titles, MSP Games brings together senior talent in game design, 3D modeling, sound engineering, and backend infrastructure. Our team culture prioritizes innovation, continuous learning, and community feedback.
